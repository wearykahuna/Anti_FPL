Overall Anti FPL Rules
Anti FPL is normal Fantasy Premier League, flipped. Every manager plays a normal FPL team as usual — but on this league table, the lowest score wins. Everything below is added on top of your normal FPL gameweek score, so the goal each week is to score as few points as possible while avoiding these penalties.

Penalties

Inactive Player
Per player in your final starting XI (after auto-subs) who played 0 minutes that gameweek. During a Bench Boost, this applies across all 15 players in your squad, not just the XI.
+9 pts each
Bank
Applied if you finish the gameweek with more than £3.0m sitting unused in the bank.
+25 pts
Transfer Hits
Per extra transfer beyond your free transfer(s). Unlike normal FPL — where a hit subtracts from your score — here it adds to it.
+4 pts each
Captain / Vice-Captain
Applied only if BOTH your captain and vice-captain play 0 minutes in the same gameweek.
+15 pts
Unused Chip
Per required chip (Bench Boost, Triple Captain, Free Hit) not used by the half-season deadline. Wildcard is exempt. Each of the 3 required chips is issued twice a season — once per half — so there are two deadlines: GW19 for the first half, and GW38 for the second.
+25 pts each

During a Live Gameweek

  • Chip penalties are never applied mid-gameweek — they're only settled at the GW19/GW38 deadlines.
  • Inactive-player and Captain/Vice-Captain penalties only get confirmed once the relevant fixtures have finished — until then they're shown as pending, not final.
  • Bank and Transfer Hit penalties apply immediately, since both are already known at the start of the gameweek.
