# Anti FPL wildcard: killing squad value

**The idea:** buy heavily-sold players right before their price falls overnight, hold them through the drop, then cycle the freed cash into the next fallers. Your squad's paper value shrinks for free while your bank ends up back where it started.

**Key rule:** the bank only needs to return to your target ITB (e.g. £3m) at the **next GW deadline** — not every day the wildcard is open. It's fine to float higher (or lower) in between.

## Mechanics
- Prices move £0.1m/day overnight (~1:30–2:30am UK), based on net transfers vs an ownership threshold.
- Max 1 move/day, 3/gameweek per player.
- Selling at or below your purchase price = no fee. Selling above = 50% fee on the profit.
- The number of players falling on any given night naturally varies — commonly 3 to 5 depending on market activity — so don't expect a fixed count each night.

## Worked example (Saturday to Saturday, £3m ITB at both ends)

| Day | Fallers overnight | Value drop | Team value | Bank (ITB) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sat (pre-WC) | – | – | £97.0m | £3.0m |
| Sat pm (WC activated) | – | – | £94.0m | £6.0m (floats) |
| Sat→Sun | 4 | −£0.4m | £93.6m | £6.0m |
| Sun→Mon | 3 | −£0.3m | £93.3m | £6.0m |
| Mon→Tue | 5 | −£0.5m | £92.8m | £6.0m |
| Tue→Wed | 3 | −£0.3m | £92.5m | £6.0m |
| Wed→Thu *(real, 27 Aug: Dowman, Watkins, Amad)* | 3 | −£0.3m | £92.2m | £6.0m |
| Thu→Fri | 5 | −£0.5m | £91.7m | £6.0m |
| Fri→Sat | 4 | −£0.4m | £91.3m | £6.0m |
| Sat (deadline) | top up ITB | – | £94.3m | £3.0m |

**Start vs end:** bank is £3.0m ITB both times — identical. But team value fell from **£97.0m to £94.3m**. That's £2.7m of squad value killed for free across the week, just by holding a fluctuating 3–5 fallers each night.

**Vs. no cycling:** rebuild once and return straight to £3m ITB → value stays at £97.0m. A full week of holding fallers is the only reason it drops to £94.3m.

## Checklist
1. Check a live predictor (FFScout, LiveFPL, OneFPL) for tonight's fallers.
2. Buy before the ~1:30am cutoff, sell only at/below cost.
3. Let the bank float mid-week — only true it up right before deadline.
4. Expect the nightly faller count to bounce around (3–5 is typical), not stay fixed.

*Only the Wed→Thu row above is real data (27 Aug 2026). All other days are illustrative.*
